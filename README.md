# Spatial Science and Urban Systems

> The structure of cities, measured.
>
> Urban spatial structure, spillovers, and neighborhood effects

SSUS is the research lab of [Elijah Knaap](https://github.com/knaaptime) at
the University of California, Irvine. We measure how cities are organized,
how influence crosses boundaries, and how place shapes outcomes. Our
methods come from spatial science: spatial econometrics, spatial
statistics, and open-source software for spatial data analysis.

## What lives here

- `ssus_paper_template`: a quarto template that builds every copy a paper
  needs over its life, formatted preprint, blinded manuscript, publisher
  class, track changes. The prose is written once; the copies are profiles.
- `ssus_course_template`: the same pattern, for a course.
- Research code and replication packages for the SSUS working papers.

## Working papers

- *Separated, Polarized, and Factioned?* — Elijah Knaap and Sergio Rey.

## Open source

Our methods ship as software, much of it in the
[PySAL](https://pysal.org) ecosystem. Code that supports a paper lands here
with the paper.
